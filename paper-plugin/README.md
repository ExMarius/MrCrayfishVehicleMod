# MrCrayfish Vehicle Plugin prototype

Paper 1.21.4 server-side port for unmodified (vanilla) clients. The current checkpoint includes the Go Kart, Lawn Mower, two-seat Quad Bike, Dune Buggy, Tractor, two-seat Dirt Bike, Moped with attachable 27-slot chest, four-seat Off Roader, two-seat Sports Car with persistent 9-slot Glove Box and 27-slot Trunk, five-seat Mini Bus, four-seat Golf Cart, ATV, two-seat Mini Bike, Smart Car, Couch, Bumper Car, Shopping Cart, two-seat Jet Ski, Speed Boat, four-seat Aluminum Boat, Sports Plane, two-seat Compact Helicopter, Sofacopter, and all five original towables: Fertilizer, Seeder, Storage Trailer, Fluid Trailer, and Vehicle Trailer. It ports the generated properties, renderer transforms, land, released aquatic, plane, and helicopter equations, charging/boosting, wheel/rotor/control-surface animation, hitch offsets, trailer following/chains, farming equipment, inventories, 100-bucket fluid tank, vehicle transport, and source openable cosmetics. Resource pack r29 added the released `1.16.X` ATV, Mini Bike, Smart Car, Speed Boat, Aluminum Boat, and Couch: their generated axle/wheel/seat/fuel-port geometry (block-texture approximations, since none of the six ship dedicated texture art in the source), the ATV's dune-buggy-style tilted-fork handlebar steering ratio, the Mini Bike's `Motorcycle`-pattern fork steering shared with the Dirt Bike/Moped (its handlebar resting position is estimated from the handles mesh's own bounding box, since the source has no generated transform for it and the fork rotation is the identity transform at rest), and each entity's own engine sample (the ATV's own sound reused by the Couch, and the Speed Boat's own sound reused by the Aluminum Boat, matching their `getEngineSound()` overrides). Resource pack r30 adds the released `1.16.X` Bumper Car and Shopping Cart, two vehicles with bespoke mechanics rather than plain driving physics: the Bumper Car's own generated body/wheel/steering-wheel geometry and its own `bonk.ogg`/`engine.ogg` samples, with car-to-car collisions approximated as a velocity jolt plus the bonk sound (its source `push()` debuffs an internal `currentSpeed` field this port does not expose, so the jolt is this port's nearest equivalent rather than a literal copy); and the Shopping Cart's own generated body geometry (reusing the mod's existing mesh/logo textures, not CFM or the legacy anvil atlas) with its push-from-behind mechanic approximated as a sneak-interact grab/release toggle that then drives the cart's position directly from the pushing player's location and yaw each tick, since this port has no walking-into-a-hitbox collision trigger of its own. (r30 also briefly shipped a Bath vehicle; it is covered below.) Resource pack r31 wired an unrelated, never-referenced `textures/vehicles/aluminum.png` file into the Aluminum Boat's hull, assuming it was the vehicle's missing dedicated art. Resource pack r33 removes Bath entirely: in the original source, Bath (like Couch and Sofacopter) is gated behind MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod being installed, but unlike Couch and Sofacopter it has no body model of its own in the Vehicle Mod's own source at all — any faithful rendering of it requires a different mod's geometry. This port's standing rule is to reproduce only the Vehicle Mod's own source and assets, so Bath (added in r30 with an ATV-body placeholder, then given recovered Furniture Mod tub geometry in r32) has been dropped rather than kept as either placeholder or cross-mod recreation; see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full history. Resource pack r34 reverts r31: the real released `1.16.X` Aluminum Boat model already specifies a plain white-concrete hull, the mod's own Cray Industries decal, and the vanilla anvil texture for its seat, all of which resolve correctly on their own, so r31's substitution was unneeded and incorrect; see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full correction. Resource pack r35 fixes the Shopping Cart's, Aluminum Boat's, and Jet Ski's Cray Industries/mesh decals rendering as missing-texture black/purple in-game: their source models' own (or, for the Jet Ski, unconverted) `vehicle:model/*`-style texture paths pointed at a non-standard folder that Minecraft's default "blocks" sprite atlas never stitches sprites from, so those five textures (the Shopping Cart's four mesh patterns and all three vehicles' Cray Industries logo) are now each copied to their own file under the pack's standard `textures/item/` folder and referenced accordingly, matching the convention already used successfully by every other vehicle's custom decals (e.g. the Off Roader's own logo and mesh grill); see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full root-cause analysis. Resource pack r36 declares the Vehicle Trailer's two side rails' true authored texture canvas size so their 17-model-pixel UV mapping (consistent across all four of each rail's faces, exceeding the 16x16 vanilla texture this port substitutes for the source's own dedicated art) scales correctly onto the substitute sprite instead of overflowing past its edge, and — per explicit user direction, as a disclosed deviation from the literal source value rather than a reproduction of it — corrects two mismatched UV faces on the Golf Cart's roof strut that match neither each other nor the element's own geometry, an isolated authoring error in the source's own asset; see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full analysis of both. Resource pack r37 fixes two further defects found from user-reported in-game symptoms: the shared `standard_wheel` item (used by every land vehicle's and trailer's wheels) authors its UV against its own declared 32x32 canvas confined to the small drawn corner of `wheel.png`, but this port's conversion dropped that declaration, doubling the effective sampled fraction and reading into the texture's transparent region — the "purple pixels" users saw under wheels; and the ATV's body/handles had been substituted with `lime_concrete`/`gray_concrete`/`light_gray_concrete` with no documented reason, rendering the dyeable default-white ATV green, now restored to the vendored source model's own `white_concrete`/`anvil`/`black_concrete`/`stone` choices; see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full analysis of both. A third reported symptom — an idle boat sitting lower in the water than while moving — was investigated and found to be the mod's own genuine released `BoatEntity` behavior (`waterLevel - 0.35 + 0.25 * min(1, speed/maxSpeed)`, already documented in r21); the user explicitly asked for it to be changed anyway, so a disclosed deviation made every boat always sit at its old full-throttle height regardless of speed, then nudged one more model pixel lower per further feedback. A follow-up round also widened the Shopping Cart's push gap from 1.3 to 1.8 blocks, since pushing it visually put the player inside the basket instead of standing behind its handle, and fixed a genuine "infinite flight" exploit where a player who was still registered as the cart's pusher could also mount its seat, forming a feedback loop between the pusher logic (which drives the cart from the player's own location) and the server's native passenger sync (which drives the player from the seat's location). A third round of user feedback narrowed the remaining purple-pixel report to a specific spot: the Vehicle Trailer's two side rails (the "tubes" visible under its sides). Resource pack r38 found and fixes the real cause: those two rails author their UV 1:1 against their own 17-model-pixel length, one unit past the vanilla 16x16 canvas this port substitutes for the source's own dedicated "frame" art, and Minecraft's own model-format documentation states UV outside 0-16 has "inconsistent" results. r36 had tried to fix exactly this by declaring `texture_size: [17, 17]` on the model, assuming the game would rescale the UV to fit — but that field is Blockbench-only editing metadata the game never reads at all, so r36's fix silently did nothing and the overflow (and the purple pixels) remained. r38 instead bakes the needed 16/17 scale directly into those two rails' own UV numbers, which actually changes what gets sampled regardless of what the game engine does or doesn't honor; the substituted texture is a flat solid color, so the sub-pixel shift this introduces is invisible. The same re-investigation also reconsidered the still-open wheel "purple pixel" report under this corrected understanding of `texture_size` and found no equivalent defect there: the shared `standard_wheel` item's own UV already stays within 0-11 (the drawn corner of `wheel.png`'s 32x32 canvas) and Minecraft always scales a model's 0-16 UV proportionally against the texture's real resolution regardless of any `texture_size` declaration, so that model was already rendering correctly both before and after r37. With the real bug condition now understood precisely (a face's UV exceeding its own *real, resolved destination texture's* pixel resolution, never mind what any model claims to declare), the pack's previously-deferred 56-model follow-up list is now closed: a permanent regression test checks every one of the pack's model files' every UV face against its real destination texture file (reading vanilla textures as the ordinary 16x16 sprites they are, and every custom `vehicle:` texture's own PNG header) and finds zero remaining overflows pack-wide. See [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for the full analysis of all three plus this closing audit. The Couch reuses Sofacopter's CFM red-sofa body and therefore shares the same hard dependency on MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod assets as the original source; see [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md) for that and other flagged mechanics. It retains r28's released Dune Buggy, r27's recovered Sofacopter rotor, r25's accepted Compact Helicopter, and r24's Sports Plane compatibility conversions without substituting fallback geometry. The complete matrix and equation audit is recorded in [`SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md`](SOURCE_POSITION_AUDIT.md).


The engine controller uses the original OGG assets, original pitch equations and 0.2 interpolation. Because a vanilla client does not expose Forge's continuously mutable `TickableSound`, the server replays each source sample at its actual pitch-adjusted duration and attaches it to the moving vehicle entity. Vehicles whose source explicitly has no engine sound, including the Sofacopter, remain silent. This avoids invented audio and the former overlapping fixed-position 18-tick impulses while remaining honest about the protocol limitation.

The current rider-pose implementation mounts every seat occupant on a minimum-scale invisible living carrier carried by the existing smooth display anchor. This asks an unmodified client to use its native mounted posture while preserving each source seat position and the accepted rider-height correction. The technical carrier is deliberately not an `AbstractHorse`: Paper 1.21.4 can loop indefinitely in its horse-only dismount-location search when a scaled carrier is invalidated. Carriers exist only while a seat is occupied and are removed on dismount. Entity-wide kill selectors are rewritten automatically: the plugin adds `tag=!mcv_plugin_vehicle` and `type=!minecraft:player` to the `/kill @e` target, protecting vehicle rigs and players while allowing the command to remove other entities. Players riding an unprotected ordinary mount are detached before that mount can be killed.

The vanilla mount-heart HUD is retained in resource pack r38 and acts as a shared ten-heart fuel gauge. Every occupied seat receives the same value from its vehicle's persisted fuel level, so the driver and passengers see matching hearts. Half-hearts round down, making the first fuel use visible immediately; the final half-heart represents empty because a living carrier cannot remain alive at zero health. A source-style action-bar overlay also reports the continuously changing `Fuel: current / capacity (percent)` value to every occupant. As in the original mod, Creative-mode drivers do not consume fuel. Ordinary horses retain their normal health display.

## Build

Requires Java 21 and Gradle 9+.

```bash
./gradlew -p paper-plugin clean build resourcePack
```

Outputs:

- `paper-plugin/build/libs/MrCrayfishVehiclePlugin-0.1.0-SNAPSHOT.jar`
- `paper-plugin/build/MrCrayfishVehiclePlugin-resource-pack-1.21.4-r38.zip`

## FalixNodes deployment

The deployment job reads `FALIX_SFTP_HOST`, `FALIX_SFTP_PORT`,
`FALIX_SFTP_USERNAME`, and `FALIX_SFTP_PASSWORD` exclusively from GitHub
Actions repository secrets. It runs only while the temporary
`.github/falix-deploy-request` marker exists. The job also synchronizes the same
pack URL, SHA-1, stable pack ID, prompt, and required flag into `server.properties`
and keeps a remote backup before any change. Paper then supplies the pack once
during login; when the plugin detects that same server-level URL, it suppresses
its delayed post-join send so clients do not perform two resource reloads. Secret
values must never be committed, written to release notes, or supplied as workflow
inputs.

## Test

1. Install the JAR in a Paper 1.21.4 server's `plugins/` directory.
2. Host the resource-pack ZIP at a direct HTTPS URL.
3. Put the URL and SHA-1 in `plugins/MrCrayfishVehiclePlugin/config.yml`.
4. Restart and use `/vehicle spawn <type>` as an operator. Tab completion lists all twenty-one vehicles and five trailers.
5. Right-click a vehicle to drive. Use W/S, A/D, Space for the handbrake, and Shift to dismount.
6. Spawn a Moped and verify its source-positioned body, cosmetics, steering-linked handles/mud guard/front wheel, lean, fuel filler, and original engine sample. Right-click it while holding a vanilla Chest to attach storage; matching the source, attachment does not decrement the selected chest stack. Click the rear chest to open its 27 slots, or sneak-right-click that chest to detach it and drop its contents.
7. On the Dirt Bike, verify both seats, steering-linked handles/front wheel, wheel spin, speed-dependent body lean, full-block traversal, exhaust, and the original engine sample.
8. Spawn an Off Roader and verify all four seats, large wheels, steering wheel, source fuel-filler position, wheelie/boost behavior, and the original shared Jet Ski engine sample. Approach and right-click it from the rear to select the closest free rear seat. Because vanilla cannot render the source standing/hanging limb pose, both rear riders intentionally use the same lower Y and accepted horse-style posture.
9. Spawn a Sports Car and verify its base plus all seven default cosmetics, two source-positioned seats, four 1.4-scale wheels, rendered large engine, steering wheel, fuel filler, charging/boosting, and original engine loop. Right-click the hood, either door, or boot to run the source 12-tick eased opening action with the original sounds. Open the boot, then target the storage region behind it to access the 27-slot Trunk; target the passenger-side dashboard region for the 9-slot Glove Box. Verify both inventories and open states survive restart/hibernation and Vehicle Trailer transport.
10. Spawn a Mini Bus and verify all five source seats, body and eight default cosmetics, four 1.19-scale wheels, fuel filler, ignition position, original engine loop, and the source oversized tow bar. The front doors open over 12 ticks and the left sliding door over 20 ticks with source easing/sounds. Hitch every compatible trailer and verify the `-25`-pixel source hitch offset. The source repository references a missing dedicated steering-wheel asset; the Paper port intentionally uses the original Go Kart wheel identified by the source Mini Bus ray transforms rather than allowing a purple/black fallback.
11. Spawn a Golf Cart and verify the complete 86-element original body, four wheels, steering wheel, fuel and ignition transforms, front seats, and two rear-facing rear seats. The original entity class is an unfinished `HelicopterEntity` subclass that cannot provide working land motion; this port explicitly applies the generated axle, wheel, steering, electric-power and seat properties to the established source land equations. It retains the unusual helicopter-rotor sample selected by those generated properties rather than substituting an invented motor sound.
12. Spawn a Jet Ski in water and verify its complete 42-element body, two seats, small fuel filler, steering-linked Quad Bike handles, splash/bubble wake, original engine loop, source surface-height rise with speed, underwater buoyancy, and carried momentum after leaving the water. The 1.16.X-dev `BoatEntity#updateVehicleMotion` is empty; r21 restores the last complete released 1.16.X water-state and buoyancy equations rather than pretending that unfinished method was functional.
13. Spawn a Sports Plane on a clear runway. Use forward/back for throttle, left/right for ground steering and aileron roll, jump for elevator-up, and sprint for elevator-down. Verify source takeoff speed, pitch/roll/yaw flight, three wheels, propeller/aileron/elevator/joystick animation, original engine loop, fuel use, safe collision handling, and persisted airborne state.
14. Spawn a Compact Helicopter with `/vehicle spawn compact_helicopter`. Use W/S for forward/backward flight, A/D for strafing, Space to ascend, and Sprint to descend; turn normally by looking while airborne. Verify the two source seats, source-following body lean/yaw, main and tail rotor pivots, interpolated joystick, original rotor loop, transformed exhaust smoke, dirt/water downwash, fuel use, landing, collision handling, and persisted blade speed/velocity. Player camera/body yaw must remain unrestricted.
15. Spawn a Sofacopter with `/vehicle spawn sofacopter` and use the same helicopter controls. Verify the official red-sofa body, source rotor mast, `1.5`-scale four-blade CFM ceiling-fan rotor at the exact two-block pivot (no aircraft wings or aluminum boat), source fuel-filler position, one source seat, 40,000-unit tank, 15-power flight behavior, persistence, and unrestricted camera yaw. The source Sofacopter intentionally has no engine/rotor sound and no Compact-specific exhaust/downwash; the Paper port remains silent rather than inventing audio or effects.
16. Sneak-right-click a trailer to pull it, then right-click a Lawn Mower, Quad Bike, Tractor, Mini Bus, or Storage Trailer to hitch it. Press Shift again to release a player-pulled trailer at its current position, matching the original crouch-to-release behavior. Storage Trailer is the chain-capable trailer.
17. Right-click Fertilizer/Seeder/Storage Trailer to open its inventory. Fertilizer accepts bone meal, Seeder accepts crop seeds, and farming equipment can consume supplies through an upstream Storage Trailer. While planting or fertilizing, verify that the visible seed/bone-meal pile remains attached to the trailer instead of jumping outward when an item is consumed.
18. Use water, lava, or powder-snow buckets on Fluid Trailer. Its capacity is 100 buckets.
19. Sneak-right-click an unoccupied vehicle to carry it, then sneak-right-click Vehicle Trailer to load it, matching the original pickup flow. To unload it, sneak-right-click the carried vehicle and right-click the ground.
20. Operators can refill the nearest powered vehicle with `/vehicle refuel` and remove the nearest vehicle/trailer with `/vehicle remove`.
21. **New, not a port:** fuel stations are an admin/roleplay fixture, not a survival block. An operator looks at a block and runs `/vehicle station create` to register it there (`/vehicle station remove` un-registers the nearest one, `/vehicle station list` prints every registered one). A player right-clicks the station to pick up its nozzle, then right-clicks a nearby vehicle to start fueling it — the hose visibly bends from the station to the player's hand every tick. Fuel adds in small increments (`fuel-station.fill-rate-percent-per-second` of the tank per second) while money is deducted per increment through [Vault](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/vault.34315/) (`fuel-station.price-per-percent` per 1% of the tank, live-adjustable with `/vehicle station price <value>`). Fueling stops the instant the player's balance, the tank space, or `fuel-station.max-distance` runs out, or either party right-clicks again. If Vault or an economy plugin isn't installed, stations dispense fuel for free instead of blocking play. Every station part (cabinet, nozzle, hose) renders from real vanilla block textures rather than any custom art.

The Forge project remains intact as the source of truth. Each Paper vehicle uses its original collision dimensions and property transforms with vanilla-style axis collision/stepping. The accepted Go Kart retains its 1.05-block step height so it can traverse a full block; the Dirt Bike uses the source land-vehicle one-block step height.
